# Changelog

## 1.0 (November 2026)

- First public release for the Bench Crow RWR display.
- Sends the radar warning picture from DCS World to the display over your home network (no address to enter).
- Threat symbols for common SAMs (SA-2/3/6/8/10/11/15/19, Hawk, Patriot, Roland and more), AAA,
  airborne threats (shown by aircraft number, e.g. MiG-29 → 29), ships and laser warnings.
- Works alongside other DCS export add-ons.
